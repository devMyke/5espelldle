    How to play

    Guess today's spell from the SRD 5.1. Each guess shows how its properties compare with the answer.

    • Green is an exact match.
    • Yellow is a partial match: some components, classes, damage types or saves overlap.
    • Red is no match. An arrow means the answer's level, casting time, range or duration is higher (▲) or lower (▼).

    You have 10 spell slots. After 4 guesses you can reveal a clue from the spell's description. A new spell appears at midnight, your time.
