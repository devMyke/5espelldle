    How to play

    Guess today's spell. Each mode has its own daily spell, so you can play all of them. Each guess shows how its properties compare with the answer.

    • Green is an exact match.
    • Yellow is a partial match: some components, classes, damage types or saves overlap.
    • Red is no match. An arrow means the answer's level, casting time, range or duration is higher (▲) or lower (▼). On Book, ▲ means the answer comes from a newer book.

    Books run in release order: PHB, SCAG, Xanathar's, Tasha's, Fizban's, Strixhaven. Class lists are as printed in each book, without Tasha's optional additions. BG3 mode uses tabletop stats, and accepts BG3 names like Bone Chill.

    You have 10 spell slots. After 4 guesses you can reveal a clue. A new spell appears at midnight, your time.
